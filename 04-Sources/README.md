# Shared sources

Store materials and extracts that may be shared with the team. Describe provenance, scope and limitations. Sources may include approved interview extracts, experiment data or a person's account of a decision.

Link to external sources from knowledge pages when copying is unnecessary. If the original is restricted, provide a shareable extract or attributed account that readers can understand without access to private files.

Do not treat a derived extract as independent corroboration of its original.
